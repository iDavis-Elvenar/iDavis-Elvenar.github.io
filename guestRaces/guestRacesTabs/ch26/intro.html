..:: The Emancipation Machine ::..
..:: Intro ::..

A group of Alchemists arrives in town, led by the eccentric Professor Zanigeeg. His advice is simple: stop playing the game of the Gods, and trust neither Enar nor Vallorion.

Instead, the Alchemists have a bold plan: build a giant magical machine that unlocks the power of the elements for your people. There's just one catch... it can only work with the help of the Elementals.

Will the Elementals agree to help, and can this machine truly set you free? Find it all out in this new chapter in the story of Elvenar!
..:: What's new in this chapter? ::..
Dragon Steel
Dragon Steel is a new Discovered Resource (similarly to Nox and Finity Gear). It is produced inside Armories from level 48, where it is added next to the existing productions of Orcs and Finity Gear.

After unlocking the very first technology of this chapter, you will be able to see Dragon Steel in the "Discovered Resources" column:


For producing Dragon Steel you will need Nox, so having a good reserve of it will be very helpful.


Similarly to other Discovered resources, Dragon Steel will decay every night.
