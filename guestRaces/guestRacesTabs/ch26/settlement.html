..:: The Emancipation Machine ::..
..:: Settlement ::..
As we have described in the Intro tab, Chapter 26 - The Emancipation Machine introduces Dragon Steel, a brand-new discovered resource produced in Armories. Dragon Steel will be required to unlock technologies of this chapter and to construct various settlement buildings. We will describe all details of new buildings in this overview.

..:: Buildings ::..

The settlement of this chapter consists of the following buildings:

  • Machine Core - the main building of this settlement. Does not need a road connection. Stores the settlement resources. Upgrading it further increases the Vials Capacity and the overall production of the settlement.
    Level 1 Level 2 Level 3 Level 4
    6x6 6x6 6x6 6x6
    Machine Core - Level 1
    Machine Core does not offer any productions, so it only serves as a central hub of your settlement and stores resources. It does not require road connection. However, the settlement buildings will surround it on all sides, so make sure you carve out a spacious area around it. To get some ideas, visit the Layouts tab, or try your own layouts in the City Planner or check the community creations in Chapter Strategies.

  • Energy Pipes - Water, Fire, Earth and Wind. Connects Energy Generators with the Machine Core. Each pipe requires 440K mana, 570 nox, 280 finity gear and 144 dragon steel.
  • Energy Generators - produce Water, Fire, Earth and Wind energy, respectively. They need to be connected via respective Energy Pipes with the Machine Core. Their production can be increased with the help of set bonuses (when attaching Engine Rooms to it).

    Water Engine Room
    ..:: Productions ::..

    Water Engine Room offers the following productions:
    • Vials - requires Mana
    • Red-Hot Ladle - requires Vials, Fire Energy and Ascended Goods
    • Root Shovel - requires Vials, Earth Energy and Canned Goods
    • Magical Manometer - requires Vials, Wind Energy and Ascended Goods

    Place the Water Engine Room directly next to other Energy Generators to increase their production.
    Note that the production values may differ in your city, depending on your Ancient Wonders and other effects. To replicate your exact values, visit the City Planner, import your city and check the production values of this settlement building.
    Take a look at the Layouts tab for various layouts to maximize the productions of your settlement.
